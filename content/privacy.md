# Privacy Policy for Good Spark

**Last Updated: November 22, 2026**

---

## Overview

Good Spark is a daily-affirmations app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

> **Our Privacy Promise**
>
> Good Spark has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you write or photograph in the app stays on your device, and in your own iCloud if you sync it. We never see it.

---

## What We Don't Collect

- No user accounts or registration
- No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs
- No user or device identifiers
- No location data
- No usage statistics
- No contacts, health or financial information

The App Store privacy label for Good Spark is "Data Not Collected".

---

## Data Stored on Your Device

Everything below is stored on your device, using standard Apple storage, and some of it in your own iCloud if you use iCloud sync (see below). We cannot see any of it.

- **Settings and history**: your preferences, the affirmations you have seen, favorites, streaks, and your first name if you choose to enter one for greetings.
- **Reflections**: the words you write about an affirmation and any photo you add to it. Photos are re-encoded on your device when you add them, which removes camera metadata such as the location where the photo was taken. Reflections are kept in the app's private storage, protected while your device is locked, and included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you use it). Deleting a reflection removes it from this device and, if you use iCloud sync, from your other devices.
- **Widgets and Live Activities**: the iPhone and iPad app shares the current affirmation with its widgets through an App Group container on the same device. Live Activities are started and updated by the app on your device; no push service is used.
- **Notifications**: daily affirmations are scheduled as local notifications on your device.
- **Purchase status**: managed by Apple's StoreKit.

Good Spark does not send this data to any server of ours.

---

## iCloud Sync

Good Spark can keep your History, favorites and reflections in step across your devices through your own iCloud account. Sync is on when you're signed in to iCloud, and you can turn it off any time with "Sync with iCloud" in the app's Settings.

- **History and favorites**: the affirmations you have seen, your favorites, which affirmations you have already seen (so they don't repeat) and when you cleared History sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through iCloud key-value storage.
- **Reflections**: the words and photo of each reflection sync between your iPhone and iPad through the private CloudKit database of your iCloud account. The text of a reflection is stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields.
- **Your iCloud, not ours**: this data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's [iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/) and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Good Spark has no server that receives it, and we cannot see it.
- **Other accounts**: when your device signs in to a different iCloud account, it shows that account's History and reflections and sends none of the previous account's. Reflections the previous account had not received yet stay on the device until that account signs in again.
- **Deleting**: deleting a reflection, or clearing History, on one device removes it from your other devices too. To remove your reflections from iCloud altogether, delete Good Spark's data in your device's iCloud storage settings; reflection sync then turns itself off instead of uploading them again.

## Speak and Writing Help

- **Speak**: when you tap Speak to dictate a reflection, Good Spark uses your microphone and Apple's speech recognition on your device. The audio is turned into text on the device; it is not stored, and neither the audio nor the text is sent to us or to Apple's servers. Speak appears only where on-device recognition is available.
- **Ideas and Keep going**: on devices with Apple Intelligence, these suggest questions to reflect on or a sentence to continue with. They run on Apple's on-device language model; your reflection is not sent to us, to Apple's servers or to Private Cloud Compute. The suggestions are only suggestions: nothing is added unless you choose it.

---

## Camera and Photos

- **Camera**: used only when you tap "Take Photo" to add a photo to a reflection. The photo is stored with that reflection on your device, and in your own iCloud if you use iCloud sync.
- **Choosing a photo**: uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick. Good Spark does not read your photo library.
- **Saving images**: when you save a share card, Good Spark asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It cannot read your library.

---

## Sharing

When you share a card or a reflection print, the image or text goes to the app or service you choose through the iOS or macOS share sheet, a link to that service, or the clipboard. Shared prints contain your photo and the affirmation, never the words of your reflection. What happens next is governed by that service's privacy policy.

---

## Feedback

"Send Feedback" opens a message in your own email app, addressed to support@good-spark.com, which you can read and edit before sending. The app itself sends nothing. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to read and reply to your message.

---

## Ratings

Good Spark may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple; we do not see who rated the app.

---

## In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We learn only whether Premium is active on your Apple Account, through StoreKit. We do not receive your name, email address or payment information. See the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## Content Updates

Good Spark downloads affirmations and the pack catalog from our content service at `vibecheck-api.vercel.app`, hosted by Vercel. Requests contain the language you use, the content tier (free or Premium) and the packs requested. They contain no account, device or user identifier. Like any web server, the hosting provider receives your IP address and standard request headers to deliver the response and protect the service. See the [Vercel Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
